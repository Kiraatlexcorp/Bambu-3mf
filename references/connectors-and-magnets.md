# Connectors, Fits & Magnets

## Clearance rules (FDM, 0.4 mm nozzle)

- Sliding / removable fit: +0.25 to +0.35 mm total
- Light press fit: +0.10 to +0.15 mm
- Magnet pocket: +0.15 to +0.20 mm on diameter, +0.1 mm on depth

## Pin + Hole (most common & reliable)

- Male pin on one part, matching hole on the other.
- Typical pin length: 4–8 mm.
- Add a 0.3–0.5 mm chamfer on the pin tip for easy insertion.
- Optional: D-shape or flat on the pin for orientation.

## Snap fits

- Cantilever hooks work well in PLA and PETG.
- Beam thickness ≥ 1.2 mm.
- Add a lead-in chamfer.

## Screw bosses

- Prefer heat-set inserts (M2 / M3) for repeated assembly.
- Printed threads are possible but wear faster in PLA.
- Boss outer diameter ≈ 2× screw major diameter.
- Boss height ≥ 2.5× diameter.

## Magnets

Common sizes:

| Magnet   | Pocket diameter | Pocket depth | Notes               |
|----------|-----------------|--------------|---------------------|
| 6 × 2 mm | 6.2–6.3 mm      | 2.1–2.2 mm   | Gridfinity standard |
| 6 × 3 mm | 6.2–6.3 mm      | 3.1–3.2 mm   | Stronger hold       |
| 8 × 3 mm | 8.2–8.3 mm      | 3.1–3.2 mm   | Larger parts        |
| 10 × 3 mm| 10.2–10.3 mm    | 3.1–3.2 mm   | Heavy lids/covers   |

- Leave a thin wall (0.8–1.2 mm) behind the magnet if it is on an outer surface.
- Decide polarity in advance (attract or repel).
- Glue (CA or epoxy) is still recommended even with a good pocket.

## Easy-fitting patterns

- Hole on one part + matching extruded pin on the other (classic “align and push”).
- Stepped shoulders for positive stop.
- Slight draft (1°) on long pins helps assembly and release.

When the user asks for “easy fitting” or “connect both parts”, default to a pin+hole pair with the clearances above unless they specify otherwise.
